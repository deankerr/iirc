# A Preview's card has one disclosure

A link card is collapsed or expanded, and nothing else. Expanded shows its words whole and its media large, together, and its media plays only where it is expanded: a thumbnail that plays expands the card and plays in the one press, and collapsing unmounts the media, which stops it. A card whose link is itself media has no disclosure at all.

The card it replaced had two switches, each held where it was drawn: a description pressed open, and a thumbnail pressed into the media large, which could not be pressed back. Together they made states nobody designed, such as a description open over a thumbnail, or media that could only be closed by hiding the whole Preview, and every new kind of media had to be fitted to both. One reversible switch leaves two states to draw, each designed, and gives the keyboard and assistive technology one control that says which the card is in.

## Consequences

- Whether a card is expanded is kept by its link beside what the toggle after the link opened, so a row drawn again is drawn as it was. Whether its media plays is the card's own, so a row drawn again plays nothing.
- The card is the only switch only when it has more to show: media that has not failed, or words cut to fit. Otherwise it is inert, and nothing offers to expand it.
- The toggle after a link still hides or shows the whole Preview. It and the card's switch must never look alike.
